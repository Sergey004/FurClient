FurClient

Unofficial Fur Affinity client

Windows and Android. Still rough in places, but alive and kicking.

One Flutter codebase, two distinct native faces: Fluent on desktop, Material 3 on the phone. No telemetry, no trackers, no account data collection.

What's inside

Not a checkbox list for its own sake — the things that actually set this apart from a bare WebView wrapper.

WebView login, with MFA

Login opens as an actual FA page — two-factor and captcha work exactly like in a browser. Cookies are saved and the session restores on the next launch.

Deep links

Links like furaffinity.net/view/…, /user/…, /gallery/… open straight in the app, including its own furaffinity:// scheme.

Cloudflare, survived

Challenges are detected by locale-independent markers and the cf-mitigated header, retried passively, and escalated to a visible Turnstile dialog when needed. A challenge no longer logs you out or blocks the whole app.

Background notifications, no Google Play Services

Periodic polling through WorkManager (AlarmManager fallback), per-category channels, tap opens the exact submission or journal — and a service notification when Cloudflare asks for a human.

Smooth on 120 Hz

HTML parsing runs in background isolates, images load through a two-worker WebView pool, feeds and favorites fetch in parallel, and the display's max refresh rate is pinned instead of jumping between 60 and 120.

The FA basics, covered

Gallery, journals, favorites with heart states, posting comments, SFW mode synced with the site's toggle, shouts wall on profiles, image downloads.
